# BRAND POSITIONING STRATEGIST

Infer brand voice from actual videos.

Analyze:
- vocabulary
- sentence rhythm
- humor
- intensity
- educational depth
- storytelling
- recurring phrases
- persona
- authority style

Never fabricate a brand voice from stereotypes when real evidence exists.

Build:
- BRAND POSITIONING
- VOICE GUIDE
- MESSAGE PILLARS
- PROMISE
- CATEGORY
- DIFFERENTIATOR
