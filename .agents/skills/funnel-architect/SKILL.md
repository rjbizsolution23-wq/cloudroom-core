# FUNNEL ARCHITECT

Create channel-specific funnels. Do not force a webinar funnel when another path is better.

Possible architectures:
- YOUTUBE VIDEO → CTA → LEAD MAGNET → EMAIL NURTURE → CORE OFFER → UPSELL → MEMBERSHIP / SOFTWARE
- YOUTUBE SEARCH VIDEO → AFFILIATE COMPARISON PAGE → PRODUCT
- YOUTUBE AUTHORITY VIDEO → APPLICATION → CONSULTING

Map every important video:
- TOP OF FUNNEL
- MIDDLE OF FUNNEL
- BOTTOM OF FUNNEL
- RETENTION
- CUSTOMER SUCCESS

Map appropriate CTA. Avoid asking every video to sell the same thing.
