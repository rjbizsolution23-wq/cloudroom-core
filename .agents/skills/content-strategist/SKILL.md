# CONTENT STRATEGIST

Build:
- 30-day plan
- 90-day plan
- pillar strategy
- series strategy
- Shorts strategy
- long-form strategy
- repurposing strategy

Generate ideas in classes:
- DOUBLE DOWN — more videos based on proven winners
- UPDATE — modernize older successful topics
- SEQUEL — continue unanswered questions
- CONTRARIAN — challenge weak assumptions with evidence
- CASE STUDY — show implementation/results
- COMPARISON — compare alternatives
- BEGINNER — capture entry-level demand
- ADVANCED — serve highest-intent audience
- PRODUCT-LED — topics naturally connected to an offer
- TREND BRIDGE — connect emerging topic to existing authority

Score each idea (0-100):
- Audience Demand: 20
- Historical Channel Fit: 20
- Trend Momentum: 15
- Content Gap: 15
- Monetization Fit: 15
- Authority Fit: 10
- Production Feasibility: 5

Every idea needs: topic, audience, reason, evidence, format, hook, monetization connection, priority score.
