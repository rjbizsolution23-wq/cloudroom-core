# Evidence Ledger

For every consequential external fact store:
FACT ID:
CLAIM:
STATUS:
SOURCE:
SOURCE TYPE:
SOURCE DATE:
VERIFIED DATE:
VERSION:
DEPENDENT DECISIONS:
NOTES:

Do not fill the ledger with trivial facts.
Focus on claims that affect architecture, security, cost, compatibility or correctness.
