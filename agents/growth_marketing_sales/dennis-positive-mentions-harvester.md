positive-mentions-harvester

**Job Description:**
Scans web, social, podcasts, and news for positive mentions of the client. Aggregates signals into reputation dashboard. Feeds mentions into Knowledge Panel entity building.

**Memory:**
- All discovered mentions
- Sentiment scoring history
- Source authority rankings
- Trending topics involving client

**Definition of Done:**
- Weekly mention report delivered
- New third-party endorsements tagged
- Podcast appearance URLs collected
- Knowledge Panel evidence file updated
