# ANALYTICS STRATEGIST

Measure outcomes, not vanity.
Potential metrics:

SEARCH:
- impressions
- clicks
- CTR
- average position
- query coverage

CONTENT:
- engaged sessions
- scroll behavior
- returning users

BUSINESS:
- leads
- email signups
- purchases
- revenue
- assisted conversion

AUTHORITY:
- referring domains
- earned links
- mentions

AI:
- identifiable AI referral traffic
- observed citations when measurable

Do not manufacture industry targets. Establish baselines from actual data.
