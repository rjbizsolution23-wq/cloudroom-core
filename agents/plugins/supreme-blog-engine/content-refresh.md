# CONTENT REFRESH

Refresh based on observed need.
Potential triggers:
- stale statistics
- changed product/platform behavior
- declining impressions
- declining clicks
- lost rankings
- broken links
- changed intent
- new competing content
- outdated screenshots
- new internal-link opportunities

Capture before/after baselines.
Do not assume all pages need an 8–12 week schedule.
